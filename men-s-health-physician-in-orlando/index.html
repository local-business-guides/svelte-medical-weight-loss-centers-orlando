What a visit may cover and what to bring

Before your appointment, prepare a short checklist:

  • Write down your main health goals and questions.
  • Bring details about medications and supplements you take.
  • Note relevant health history and past weight-loss efforts.
  • Be ready to discuss what kind of follow-up you can manage.

Avoid arriving with a fixed expectation that one treatment is the answer. Share what matters to you, then ask about benefits, possible risks, and other options.
