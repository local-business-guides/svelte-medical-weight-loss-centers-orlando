Questions to ask before starting

Use your first call or appointment to get a clear picture of the process. It may help to have this checklist nearby:

  • Describe the outcome you’re hoping for and what you’ve already tried.
  • Ask how a provider assesses which program or treatment may fit your needs.
  • Mention medical concerns, current treatments, and questions about medications.
  • Find out what information you should bring and what the next step would be.

Avoid choosing a treatment based only on someone else’s experience, expecting a specific medication, or leaving out details that could matter to your care. Don’t change a current prescription without guidance from your clinician. If you’re unsure which kind of support to seek, explain that when you contact the center and ask how to begin.
